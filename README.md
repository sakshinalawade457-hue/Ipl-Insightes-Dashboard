# 🏏 IPL 2023 Performance Insights Dashboard

An interactive, analytical Power BI dashboard designed to evaluate team performances, match outcomes, target scores, and venue distributions for the **IPL 2023 season**. 

Featuring a custom dark luxury layout, this report turns complex tournament data into clear, actionable visual insights.

---

## 📊 Key Highlights & KPI Metrics

* **Total Matches Evaluated:** `37 Matches`
* **Total Venues:** `11 Stadiums` across major Indian cities
* **Franchise Participation:** `10 Teams`
* **Highest Target Score Recorded:** `235 Runs`

---

## 💡 Detailed Insights & Findings

### 🏆 1. Team Dominance (Win Count Analysis)
* **Top Performers:** **Chennai Super Kings (CSK)**, **Gujarat Titans (GT)**, and **Rajasthan Royals (RR)** led the win counts in the analyzed segment, securing the highest number of successful match finishes.
* **Middle Pack:** **Lucknow Super Giants (LSG)**, **Punjab Kings (PBKS)**, and **Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB)** maintained consistent competitive performances, remaining within close contention for playoff spots.
* **Challenged Teams:** **Delhi Capitals (DC)** and **Sunrisers Hyderabad (SRH)** experienced a tougher campaign, recording fewer wins due to lower success rates during high-target run chases.

### 🎯 2. Match Result & Win Distribution
* **Donut Chart Share:** Displays the proportional match wins across all franchises, highlighting a balanced distribution among top-tier teams versus lower-ranked teams.
* **Target Benchmarks:** The highest target score in the analyzed dataset reached `235`, reflecting high-scoring pitch conditions at key venues such as M. Chinnaswamy Stadium and Eden Gardens.

### 🏟️ 3. Venue & City Impact
* Multi-venue dynamic filtering reveals that home ground conditions heavily influenced win probabilities.
* High-scoring venues (e.g., Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Kolkata) favored teams with deep batting order depth and high powerplay strike rates.

## 🛠️ Dashboard Architecture & Visual Elements

1. **Top Metric Bar:** Four standardized KPI card containers providing high-level operational statistics at a glance.
2. **Teams Win Count Bar Chart:** Horizontal bar visual utilizing brand-matched team colors to compare match victories across all franchises.
3. **Match Result Distribution:** Donut visual offering a percentage breakdown of match outcomes.
4. **Dynamic Filter Slicers:** Dual selection panels on the right-hand sidebar for filtering by **Venue Stadium** and **Venue City**.
